NON-NEGOTIABLE FAITHFUL RULES

- no normal wild Pokémon
- the same overall Colosseum story/progression backbone
- the same Shadow backbone
- the same Relic Stone-centered purification identity
- Wes + Espeon + Umbreon opening
- Double-Battle-first identity wherever Colosseum supports it
- Colosseum-style destination travel
- full Shadow system
- full purification identity
- P★DA feel
- Mt. Battle as required content

PURIFIED RULES
Purified Mode is the lore-friendly expanded mode.
It must:
- keep the main Colosseum story spine recognizable
- keep Shadow Pokémon as the core identity
- keep Relic Stone purification identity intact
- avoid turning Orre into a normal wild-route region
- use the phased Mt. Battle preserve as the main wild-catch expansion space
- remain clearly separated from Faithful
